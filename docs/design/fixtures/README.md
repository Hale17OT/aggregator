# Fictional fixtures

`articles.json` is generated by `scripts/make-fixtures.mjs` and consumed by both native Figma generation and the deterministic local adapter. Alderwick, Alderwick Ledger, Quay Notebook and the mobility office are invented. All original links use example.com and are mock targets, not verified reporting.

Cases: normal multi-source story with synthetic publisher-transcript segment; long headline and source titles; differing counting windows/units; correction with unavailable original. Four version-wide sources, three reviewed fictional origins. Shared organizer items do not add independence. Standard is 550 words and Brief 152; PRD word ranges are guides, and this bounded small story intentionally stays shorter.

Fixtures are drafts at rest. Local bootstrap simulates a reviewer and passes the policy gate into disposable memory. This is never a real human approval. The browser disclosure states that boundary. No source bodies are downloaded; grants are synthetic. The unavailable-original case retains fictional permission; revocation is tested separately and suppresses publication.
