The Dqnamo cassette reference was opened in actual Chrome at 1440 px and 390 px. Local captures: output/playwright/radio-rebuild/reference-desktop.png, reference-playing.png, reference-mobile.png. The real Play One Small Step control started its 12.953-second audio; its range advanced to 1.72 seconds and Mute was exercised. Source code was not copied. The page happens to display code beneath the object; this was not used for implementation.

The compelling details are the double shell seam, inset paper label, metal screw recesses, physically contained controls, depth of the mechanism window and sharply grounded contact shadow. The primary light is upper left. Plastic, paper and exposed mechanism read differently. Playback changes a mechanism attached to the object rather than decorating a separate dashboard. Our original radio uses these construction principles with a different silhouette, forest enamel shell, cream chassis, recessed woven speaker, smoked amber glass and tactile transport keys. There are no cassette reels or copied brand assets.

Reference seek and pressed-state/mobile observations are recorded separately in the script/captures; visual quality acceptance belongs to the owner. Functional tests do not imply comparable craftsmanship has been accepted.
